Our Story

Born From a Love of Winter

FrostWear started with a simple idea: winter clothing should never force you to choose between staying warm and looking good. Founded in Kathmandu, we set out to craft outerwear that handles the coldest mornings while keeping everyday style front and center.

Every piece in our collection is designed with intention, from carefully selected fabrics to stitching built to last season after season. What began as a small workshop has grown into a brand trusted by thousands across Nepal and beyond.
